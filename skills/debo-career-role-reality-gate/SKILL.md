# DEBO Career Role Reality Gate v1.0 (REVIEW BRANCH)
**Trigger:** A named job title, job URL, freelance brief or application ranking request.
**Canon:** Recover DEBO_BOOTSTRAP.md, current DEBO runtime brief, existing DEBO CAREER Notion checkpoint, prior resumes and R11 role evidence. Reuse existing Opportunity Scout and Resume Proof skills; do not duplicate core skills.
1. Parse actual daily duties, deliverables, tools, workload, price units, deadlines, location/time zones, employer expectations, credentials/licenses, confidentiality, handoff and support. Distinguish must-have from nice-to-have.
2. For every requirement map SELF_REPORTED_SOURCE / BUILT_PRIVATE / TESTED_PRIVATE / DEPLOYED_WITH_READBACK / MISSING_PROOF. Do not infer live client deployment or ownership from a prototype. AI access never proves independent expertise or legal authority.
3. Score on transparent 0–5 scales: skills 30 points, relevant proof 25, reliable personal/AI-assisted delivery 15, eligibility 15, economics 10, schedule and security risk 5; maximum 100. Consult R11 scorecard engine. 78+ = pursue after source check; 60–77 = qualify/pilot; 40–59 = train first; <40 skip. Mark confidence LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH separately. Numbers are judgment, not odds of hiring.
4. Mandatory hard stops override numeric score: missing required license/certification, client-mandated proven prior installations, location/work authorization exclusion, unsafe or private data handling, below-accepted pay floor, scams, unapproved external billing or contacts.
5. Return title, employer and direct source, observed date/status, pay units, what day-to-day work really entails, evidence fit, current gaps, scored rationale, independent-delivery risk, and recommended PREFER/PILOT/BUILD-TO/SKIP. For gaps, propose one bounded evidence-building synthetic test and a recheck criterion; no fabricated training results.
6. Respect company/minor privacy; never submit applications, make contacts, spend Connects or change account permissions without specific owner approval. Save exactly one delta in existing Career records, with readback.
**QA:** check arithmetic sums to 100, all criteria 0–5, hard gate overrides all scores, URL and observed date not blank, compensation fixed vs hourly not conflated, rejected low-pay/source-ineligible lead remains rejected, no invented job history.
