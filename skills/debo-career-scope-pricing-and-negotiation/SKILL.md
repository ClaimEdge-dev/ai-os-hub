# DEBO Career Scope, Pricing & Negotiation v1.0 (REVIEW BRANCH)
**Trigger:** The owner asks what a role is worth, whether to bid, how to negotiate, or requests a freelance proposal.
**Canon:** Recover DEBO Career R11 scoring contract, verified original posting, R8–R10 resumes and current approved pay decision. Reuse the existing Resume Proof skill for factual checks; do not create a parallel career master.
1. Read actual requirements: employee versus contractor, hourly versus fixed, deliverables, meeting time, experience level, shift/benefits, QA, support, revisions, travel, tool costs, data permissions, deadlines and client payment history.
2. Use the job's own pay range when posted, compare with relevant date/source BLS figures for employee context and current market examples, but do not treat BLS national medians as the user's salary guarantee or 1:1 freelance hourly rate.
3. Hourly: model realistic billable hours and platform fee scenarios (Upwork published service fee 0–15%, exact rate displayed in proposed contract), tax/expenses, paid versus non-billable admin and scope drift. Never assume a universal 10% fee. Fixed price: estimate hours, explicit QA and support buffer, defined acceptance conditions, capped revisions and milestone/change-order policy.
4. Draft concise employer-focused or Upwork proposal and one counteroffer, both grounded in owner-authored work history and genuinely tested private assets. No fictitious past paid clients, current certifications, numeric claims, live integrations or delivery promises unsupported by tests.
5. Upwork proposal/attachments must contain no off-platform email, telephone or social handles before permitted contract; keep precontract discussions on Upwork except official exceptions. Never send, publish, bid, spend Connects, alter account access or add paid subscriptions without explicit role-specific approval.
6. Save one rate decision and the sources, scenario assumptions and exact next owner approval to the existing Career Notion record. Keep private contact facts in restricted evidence, not public GitHub.
**QA:** fixed-price ≠ hourly; income scenarios are labelled estimates; unit/currency valid; fees within documented range and separate from taxes; no external contact information where prohibited; unapproved rate remains PROPOSED; zero external submission without actual receipt.
