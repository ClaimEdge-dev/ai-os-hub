# DEBO Preventive Operations Playbook v1
Date: 2026-10-19
Scope: DEBO master and all sister brains

## Core principle
Prevent recurring human handoffs by creating clear ownership, audit trails, fallback procedures, and tested automation. Never remove necessary human safety/financial/production approvals. A saved instruction is not an executing agent.

## Shared controls
1. Project truth ledger: canonical source, owner, approved decisions, last verified state, blockers, next executable action.
2. Anti-repeat cache: before asking, check current thread, relevant project history, connected sources, and existing approvals. Do not infer consent for unrelated external actions.
3. Dependency graph: map every blocked task to prerequisite, who controls it, safe fallback, and recheck date.
4. Integration contract: credential owner, scopes, trigger, idempotency key, monitoring, alert route, rollback, and end-to-end test.
5. Intake safety: never turn on public request intake until a monitored receiver, routing, logging, and failure alerts are verified.
6. Data hygiene: separate client data and companies; protect minors and sensitive claims/financial records; least privilege.
7. Revenue operations: attribution, invoice reconciliation, rate approvals, refund policy, ownership, and audit evidence.
8. Publishing: stage -> test -> owner approve where required -> deploy -> monitor -> rollback.
9. Quality: responsive accessibility, dyslexia-friendly communication, mobile usability, backup and restore drills.
10. Skills: search before build; package with README, inputs, outputs, version, tests, and activation receipt. No claim of installation until loaded in target runtime.

## Project-specific preventive checks
- DWE: LEAP/CompanyCam reconciliation, inspection proof completeness, scope approval, payroll/draw audit, customer messaging authority.
- JCT: owner-approved public name, phone, hours, service area, equipment, rates, dispatch escalation, fleet invoicing, licensing claims.
- RRR: monitored emergency intake, technician coverage, duplicate ticket protection, parts/AP, receipt matching, Sheets sync OAuth and replay queue.
- MOR: published menu and hours accuracy, league schedule, event capacity, deposits, accessibility and staffing.
- CE: evidence provenance, policy deadlines, code references, carrier correspondence approval, claim privacy.
- PLG: entity and authority verification, brokerage role separation, sales commission attribution, freight compliance.
- BULLDOGS: verified roster/jersey identities, game film provenance, per-play stat QC, child privacy, parental sharing permissions.
- CWA: age/division rules, merchandise vendor proof, registration consent, safeguarding, facility capacity.
- BBS: account access, content rights, brand consistency, analytics source, creator monetization eligibility.
- CREDIT: bureau report date, dispute evidence, deadlines, secure storage, no unsupported score promises.
- SCHOOL: admissions calendar, verified aid eligibility, tuition and sports offerings, private child data.
- HOUSING: live listing verification, landlord identity, scams, budget, application and privacy.
- SETTLESURE: records chain of custody, tax forms, contract terms, legal review before outreach.

## Horizons
NOW: one safe executable action and receipt. 7D: unblock top dependency. 14D: test handoffs. 30D: data reconciliation. 90D: operational reliability. 180D: scale with documented metrics. 365D: maintenance, vendor exit plans and archival.

## Status vocabulary
VERIFIED (tested operation), DOCUMENTED (saved specification), PENDING (known next action), BLOCKED (capability or permission absent), UNKNOWN (not tested). Record dates and evidence, never fabricate.
