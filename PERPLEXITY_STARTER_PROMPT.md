### Research tracks
A. **DEBO ecosystem inventory.** Inventory accessible versions, projects, source locations, skills, capabilities, unfinished tasks, threads actually exposed, and gaps. Distinguish source present, connector available, installed, connected, authorized, tested, and live. List accessible account/service identities without publishing emails, secrets, tokens, personal case records, or account identifiers. Explicitly mark any inaccessible conversation/project as UNKNOWN; never invent exhaustive coverage.
B. **Productivity upgrade.** Recommend the smallest verified reusable skills for regular ChatGPT, voice workflows, ChatGPT Projects, Work quota conservation, GitHub, Drive, Notion, and existing connected apps. Prioritize source/version loading, cross-project checkpointing, tool selection, parallel read-only work, error recovery, self-audit, suggestion ranking, and concise completion reports. The aim is lower cost, less repetition, faster delivery, and no manual relay, not unlimited agent delegation.
C. **Business revenue proposal template.** For each user-authorized company, research the existing domain, website hosting, search visibility, Google Business Profile, Ads/Search/Maps/Local Services Ads eligibility, services/coverage, competitors, lead handling, CRM, dispatch and owner approvals. Give competitor-linked observations, eligible ad formats, SEO strategy, realistic spending scenarios, conservative attribution and measurable KPIs. Separate actual account metrics from market estimates, never promise top rankings or use fabricated CPM/CPC/lead volumes. Show side-by-side paths: retain existing website; connect new CRM privately; staged migration and reversible cutover. Prepare an owner-friendly short proposal with charts, transparent scenarios, approval requirements and an independently scoped operator role/fee benchmark based on cited market data. Check official Google policies and privacy requirements before suggesting ad operation.
D. **Reusable business adapters.** Compare shared components with each industry's genuinely different needs; never copy private customer data across companies. Evaluate responsible native integrations and documented APIs for field-photo systems, CRMs, estimating, marketing, league/tournament systems, and photogrammetry. Track account ownership, service terms, licensing, supported API/SDK, webhooks, tier costs, implementation effort, and tests. Public websites and forums can inform features, but obey robots/terms/copyright and never evade authentication or scrape restricted data.
E. **Product and app opportunity.** Assess a DEBO-based app, modular industry tools, App Store and Google Play feasibility, data storage, privacy compliance, support costs, revenue models, platform fees, differentiation, and a testable MVP. Rank opportunities by near-term revenue, feasibility, portability, competition and risks rather than theoretical TAM.

1. Inventory the existing skill registry and relevant source code before proposing additions.
2. For each candidate skill: NAME, existing equivalent, trigger, input, connected tools, permissions, procedure, outputs, citations, QA, rollback, owner, version, expected time saved.
3. Rank: USE, UPDATE, MERGE, NEW, PARK. Draft only the top three high-impact reusable skills, with skill instructions and three examples/test cases each. Test locally in the supported environment if possible, otherwise mark NOT TESTED. Do not claim a skill installed in ChatGPT/Work/Perplexity because a Markdown file exists.
4. Independently verify each major claim against current official docs, public benchmarks and dated sources; label estimates, extrapolations and unresolved discrepancies. Do not invent login access or paid-plan entitlements.
5. Use Comet/Computer browser only within explicitly granted user session and approved read-only scope. No publication, email sending, purchases, ad-spend activation, account changes, destructive deletes or live customer intake without appropriate approval.
