HOW IT WORKS

Breakdown to closeout.

  1. 1. Tell us where you are.Location, unit and safe-access details.
  2. 2. Tell us what happened.Symptoms, fault codes and photos when available.
  3. 3. Rapid confirms capability.Technician, tools, parts and dispatch options are checked.
  4. 4. Work is documented.Diagnosis, authorization, parts, labor, travel and evidence.
  5. 5. Job closes cleanly.Invoice-ready record, recommendations, follow-up and fleet history.

FAQ

What drivers and fleets will ask first.

Do you come to the truck?

Private benchmark copy: yes, the business is modeled as mobile-first roadside, yard and job-site service. Final public wording remains truth-gated.

Do you work on trailers?

Trailer repair is part of the current service architecture, including electrical, air, brake, suspension, landing-gear and hardware categories.

Do you work on heavy equipment?

The private build supports heavy-equipment service. Exact equipment classes still need real capability confirmation before public launch.

Are you available 24/7?

The benchmark model assumes 24/7 emergency availability. It remains a private assumption until adopted.

How much does a road call cost?

The private benchmark model starts at a $150 service call plus $150/hour labor with a two-hour minimum. This is not an official quote or Rapid rate.

Can fleets open an account?

The system is already designed for fleet accounts, PO rules, Net-30 benchmark terms, asset rosters and recurring PM. Real account terms require setup.

Can I send photos?

The Request Service flow is designed for photo intake. Production upload remains private until secure storage is activated.

What happens if the truck cannot be repaired roadside?

The benchmark operating model is repair-first with towing coordination when on-site repair is impractical. Rapid towing ownership is not being claimed.
