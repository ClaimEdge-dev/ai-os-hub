FAQ

Straight answers before dispatch.

Do you come to the truck?

The private build is mobile-first: roadside, truck stop, yard and job-site service. Final public wording stays truth-gated until the operating model is confirmed.

Do you work on trailers?

Trailer repair is part of the private service architecture, including air, electrical, brakes, suspension, landing gear and hardware categories.

Are you available 24/7?

The benchmark model assumes 24/7 emergency availability. It remains private until formally adopted.

How much does a road call cost?

The private benchmark model uses a $150 service call and $150/hour labor with a two-hour minimum. That is not an official quote or Rapid price.

Can fleets open accounts?

The system supports account contacts, unit history, PO rules, recurring PM and a Net-30 benchmark model after credit approval.

What if it cannot be repaired on-site?

The benchmark operating model is repair-first with towing coordination when roadside repair is impractical. Rapid towing ownership is not being claimed.
