# Morgan’s Perplexity Research Router

## Use for
Current web research, competitors, hospitality practices, pool/tournament systems, local SEO, events, technology, regulations, sponsorship and consumer sentiment.

## Inputs
Decision question, known facts, source pack, date scope, geography, freshness requirement.

## Procedure
1. State the decision the research must support.
2. Separate Morgan’s verified facts from unknowns.
3. Require current primary sources first.
4. Require exact citations/URLs.
5. Require STALE / CONFLICT / UNRESOLVED reporting.
6. Return research as VERIFIED EXTERNAL or RESEARCH only.
7. DEBO verifies before any promotion to Business Truth.

## Return schema
EXECUTIVE ANSWER / SOURCES / FACTS / CONFLICTS / ASSUMPTIONS / OPTIONS / RISKS / RECOMMENDATION / NEXT VERIFICATION.

## Fail conditions
No sources, stale-only support, wrong business identity, or an answer that depends on inaccessible first-party data.
