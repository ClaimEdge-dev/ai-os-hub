# JCT Operating Scorecard
Use verified period-bounded data only. Core measures may include inbound calls, missed calls, leads, dispatches, completed jobs, revenue billed, revenue collected, average collected/job, tow class mix, heavy recovery revenue, storage revenue, AR aging, payment days, commercial revenue, lead source, conversion, reviews and contribution margin when costs exist. Clearly mark incomplete coverage and do not manufacture KPIs. Separate billed from collected revenue. Output concise owner decisions and next actions.
