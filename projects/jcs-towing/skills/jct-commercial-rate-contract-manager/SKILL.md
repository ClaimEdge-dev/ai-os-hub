# JCT Commercial Rate Contract Manager
Recover existing account terms first. Separate proposed, negotiated, executed, expired and superseded terms. Track account, service classes, rates/units, minimums, mileage, storage, after-hours, payment terms, insurance/COI requirements, effective/renewal dates and source document. Any external offer or executed change requires approval. Route pricing evidence to rate intelligence and economics to margin engine.
