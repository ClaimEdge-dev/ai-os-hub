# JCT Heavy Recovery Scene Builder

## Trigger
Use for semi, tractor-trailer, rollover, ditch, bridge, load-shift, cargo, multi-unit, or specialty-equipment recoveries.

## Capture
Scene condition; hazards; vehicle/trailer/load state; dispatch/arrival/departure; each unit/operator; stabilization; rigging; winch configuration/pulls; separation/rehook; cargo actions; cleanup; traffic/scene control; vendors; tow movements; destination; photos/video.

## Rules
- Each billed unit/hour must map to an actual task and time interval.
- Separate recovery equipment time from transport movement.
- Third-party equipment requires source/vendor evidence.
- Do not describe police/fire equipment as JC equipment.

## Output
Chronological recovery narrative, unit/operator time map, equipment map, photo-evidence plan, billing-evidence map, and unresolved gaps.
