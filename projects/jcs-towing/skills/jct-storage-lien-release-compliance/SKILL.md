# JCT Storage, Lien & Release Compliance

## Trigger
Use for stored vehicles, storage billing, insurer storage communications, lienholder/owner notices, release, abandoned vehicles, police impounds, or ICC-controlled storage.

## Rules
- Identify towing lane and governing authority before applying a storage rate or procedure.
- Preserve storage-in and storage-out timestamps.
- Separate customer/insurer communication from statutory notice obligations.
- Do not claim a lien, release condition, or notice deadline without current authority.
- External notices/releases remain approval-gated when required by JCT controls.

## Output
Storage chronology, notice checklist, release checklist, rate authority, compliance gaps, and required next action.
