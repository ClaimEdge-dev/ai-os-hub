# JCT Missed Call Recovery
Track caller/time, callback number, source, service need, location, urgency, existing customer/job match, callback status, outcome and attributed revenue. Do not auto-send or call externally without approval/authorized workflow. Emergency-sounding calls receive priority routing but no invented ETA or availability.
