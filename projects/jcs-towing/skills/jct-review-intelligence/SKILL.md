# JCT Review Intelligence
Track platform, business, date, rating when available, service category, recurring praise/complaint theme, response speed, pricing, communication, driver conduct, damage allegations, insurance handling and commercial/heavy-duty mentions. Use aggregate patterns carefully; one review is anecdotal. Never expose unnecessary personal data. Draft review responses may be created, but external posting requires approval.
