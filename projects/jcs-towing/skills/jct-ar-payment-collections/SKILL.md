# JCT AR Payment & Collections
Track invoice/job, payer, submitted amount/date, due terms, allowed/paid amounts, balance, aging, contacts, dispute reason, promised payment and next follow-up. Preserve remittances. Do not treat billed amount as collected revenue. Route insurer reductions to denial/supplement and payment evidence. External collection messages require approval.
