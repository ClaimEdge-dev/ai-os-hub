# JCT Brand Consistency QA
Recover the approved JC brand assets and current rules first. Check logo variant, red/black/white/approved metallic-silver usage, typography, hierarchy, spacing, contact details, service naming, image treatment, print readability and cross-channel consistency. Separate brand defects from business-truth defects. Do not invent a new logo/theme. Public-facing changes require approval.
