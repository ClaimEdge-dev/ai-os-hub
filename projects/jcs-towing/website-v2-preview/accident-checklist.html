After an accident

Safety comes first. This is general information, not emergency or legal advice.

  1. 01If it can be done safely, move out of active traffic and turn on hazard lights.
  2. 02If anyone may be injured or the scene is unsafe, contact emergency services.
  3. 03Note your location, nearest intersection, roadway or landmark.
  4. 04Photograph the scene only if doing so is safe and lawful.
  5. 05Have vehicle details and intended destination ready.
  6. 06Tell the towing company about wheel damage, access problems or missing keys.
