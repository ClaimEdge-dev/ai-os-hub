Vehicle breakdown

A few details can make the towing request easier to understand.

  1. 01If possible and safe, get the vehicle out of active traffic and turn on hazard lights.
  2. 02Stay aware of traffic and avoid standing in a dangerous lane or blind spot.
  3. 03Note your exact location, nearest exit, intersection or landmark.
  4. 04Have the vehicle year, make and model ready if known.
  5. 05Know where you want the vehicle taken, if possible.
  6. 06Tell the towing company whether the vehicle rolls, steers, shifts, has keys or damaged wheels.
