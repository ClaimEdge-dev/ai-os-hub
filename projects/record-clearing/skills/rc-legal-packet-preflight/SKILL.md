# Illinois Packet Preflight
Use the current Illinois Courts approved form suite, not newly invented court forms. Check official case number, county/court, identity, every charge and disposition, disposition evidence, statutory authority with effective date, waivers, notices and current form edition. Missing fields are BLOCKED, not guessed. Return a review-only checklist and a draft attorney packet. Human counsel verifies legal eligibility. No filing, letter, fee payment, signature, private sharing or release without separate client approval.
